Level Up IT

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Short version: Level Up IT collects no data. Your progress stays on your device.

What the app stores

The app saves your study progress (which cards you know, your quiz and practice-exam scores, your exam date, your reminder time, and the name and teacher email you enter). All of it is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No internet connection is used. The app works fully offline.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No data is shared with or sold to third parties.
  • Optional progress reports: if you tap "Email my progress", your own mail app opens with a short report (your name, study progress, and scores) addressed to the email you chose. Nothing is sent unless you press Send in your mail app.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device. No server is involved.

Deleting your data

Use Settings, then "Reset progress" in the app, or delete the app. Either one removes everything it stored.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions: open a request on the support page, or ask your instructor.
